ABOUT MAMIHLО

Rooted in Ghana. Made for the world.

MamiHlo is the consumer product brand of Earth & Beyond Empire Limited, an all-women-led Ghanaian social enterprise and agro-processing company.

Rooted in indigenous African excellence, MamiHlo transforms locally cultivated mushrooms and agricultural ingredients into premium, convenient, plant-based products inspired by Ghanaian culinary heritage.

The name MamiHlo brings together “Mami” — the respected woman who has always fed you well — and “Hlo,” the Ewe word for mushroom.
